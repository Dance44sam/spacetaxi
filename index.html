🚕 TINY PLANETS TAXI 🌍

pick up riders, fly them across the galaxy, earn your fare! 30 worlds to explore!

W / Up Arrow — thrust forward  •  A / D or Left / Right — steer
S / Down Arrow — back up  •  Shift — hold for a big boost (works together with thrust!)
Scroll — zoom the camera in and out
R — start a fresh shift any time, no worries!

On phone/tablet: use the left thumb pad to steer and thrust, and the orange boost button on the right — you can hold both down at once! Pinch with two fingers, or use the + / − buttons in the top right, to zoom the camera.
Your little taxi has gentle engines, so hold Shift/Boost to blast off a planet's surface.
Flying low through a planet's clouds slows you down a bit — that's the air pushing back!
Your flight path shows as a soft yellow dotted line, so you always know where you're headed.
Land gently on a glowing planet to pick up riders and drop them at their stop!
Each trip pays a fare — the farther you fly, the more you earn.
Planets marked ⛽ will fill your tank back up while you visit.
