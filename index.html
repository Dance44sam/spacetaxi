🚀 TINY PLANETS TAXI

Pick up passengers and fly them between worlds.

W / Up Arrow — thrust forward  •  A / D or Left / Right — steer
S / Down Arrow — reverse thrust  •  Shift — hold for a large boost (works together with thrust)
Scroll — zoom the camera
R — restart the shift at any time

On phone/tablet: the left thumb pad steers and thrusts, and the boost button on the right can be held at the same time. Pinch, or use the + / − buttons in the top right, to zoom.
Hold Shift/Boost for extra thrust when taking off from a planet.
Flying low through a planet's clouds adds drag and slows you down.
The dotted line shows your predicted flight path.
Land gently on a planet with waiting passengers to pick them up, then land at their destination to drop them off.
Fare scales with distance.
Planets marked ⛽ refuel your tank.
